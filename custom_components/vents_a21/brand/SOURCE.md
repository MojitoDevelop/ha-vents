# Vents brand artwork

Source: https://vents-group.pl/img/strona-glowna/logo-vents.png?v=4

The source is linked from the official https://vents-group.pl/ website's stylesheet.
Retrieved on 2026-11-13 and preserved in `assets/vents/logo-original.png` in the source repository.

`scripts/render_brand.py` crops whitespace and the emblem for the square icon,
then resizes proportionally and adds white padding for Home Assistant's brand sizes.
The full wordmark is included as `logo.png` and `logo@2x.png`.

Vents names and artwork belong to their respective owners. The repository's MIT
license covers the integration code, not the manufacturer artwork. The artwork
identifies the equipment supported by this independent community integration.
